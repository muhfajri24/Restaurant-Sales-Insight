# Restaurant Performance Intelligence

## Analytical Scope
Recorded performance from 2022-11-07 through 2022-12-29.

## Validated Results
Recorded revenue is 769,515.89 across 254 sales records and 53 active days. Burgers has the largest product share (49.0%); Lisbon has the largest city share (31.4%).

## Interpretation
Revenue is decomposed into recorded quantity and weighted selling price. Order KPIs, profit, customer preference, and causal conclusions are unavailable.

## Decision Use
Use the opportunity matrix as a list of validation hypotheses, not guaranteed recommendations.
